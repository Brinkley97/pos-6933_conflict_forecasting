Benchmark results folder
========================

csv/
  Contains the benchmark prediction files, tuning files,
  and comparison tables used to evaluate new student models
  against the benchmarks.

plots/
  Contains benchmark comparison plots copied from earlier course work.

How students should use this folder
-----------------------------------

1. Treat the benchmark files as the baseline to beat.
2. Compare student models to both benchmark models on matched rows.
3. Focus first on the csv/ tables, then use the plots for interpretation.
